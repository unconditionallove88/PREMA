FEATURE: BREATH OF LOVE

Create a premium guided breathing experience called "Breath of Love".

PURPOSE

The goal is to guide users into Coherent (Resonant) Breathing, helping synchronize heart rate, nervous system activity, blood pressure, and brainwave patterns into a coherent state associated with relaxation, emotional openness, connection, and optimal conditions for oxytocin release.

The entire experience should feel calming, loving, safe, elegant, and deeply immersive.

MOBILE-FIRST DESIGN

- Full-screen experience.
- No distracting elements.
- Minimal interface.
- Smooth transitions.
- Premium wellness aesthetic.
- Soft lighting effects.
- Fluid animations at 60fps.

Purpose:

  Blue → Green → Pink → Blue

BREATHING GUIDE

CENTERPIECE

In the center of the screen place a large luminous breathing orb.

The orb represents the user's breath.

Appearance:
- Soft glowing sphere
- Pink-blue gradient
- Multiple glow layers
- Organic living-light effect

BREATHING ANIMATION

INHALE PHASE
Duration: 5 seconds

- Orb slowly expands.
- Glow becomes brighter.
- Light gently radiates outward.
- Display text:

"INHALE"

Optional subtext:
"Breathe in love"

EXHALE PHASE
Duration: 5 seconds

- Orb slowly contracts.
- Glow softens.
- Energy gently returns inward.
- Display text:

"EXHALE"

Optional subtext:
"Release and soften"

BREATHING RHYTHM

- 5 seconds inhale
- 5 seconds exhale
- No breath holding
- Continuous cycle
- Exactly 6 breaths per minute

GUIDANCE TEXT

Display beneath the orb:

Breath of Love

Coherent (Resonant) Breathing

Synchronize your heart, mind, and breath.

Breathe gently through your nose.

Inhale for 5 seconds.
