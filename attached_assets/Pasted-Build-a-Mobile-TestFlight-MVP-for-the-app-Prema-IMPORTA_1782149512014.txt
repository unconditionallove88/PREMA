Build a Mobile TestFlight MVP for the app "Prema". 

IMPORTANT CONCEPTUAL CHANGE: The app structure is now defined by three psychological phases rather than time-based ones. This "Triad of Prema" (Intention, Action, Attention) applies to both this mobile MVP and the full web version of the project.

PHILOSOPHY:
- Prema is a companion for nightlife, festivals, and clubs.
- Focus: Self-love, Presence, Honesty, Respect, and Responsibility.
- No "fear-based" language. Replace the concept of "Safety" with "Care" and "Presence".
- Tone: Warm, human, grounded, and supportive. Like a caring friend.
- Visuals: Use existing Prema web design, color palette, and elegant UI styles.

---

### PHASE 1: INTENTION (Preparation of Mind and Space)

**Prema (Setting the Vibe)**
- Before heading out, the user selects an intention (e.g., Be Here Now, Self-Acceptance, Honesty, Respect, Gratitude).
- This intention acts as a subconscious anchor for the experience.

**Essentials Kit**
A checklist of items to bring for self-care:
- Phone (100% Charged) — Your lifeline to your Circle of Love
- Single-Use Straws — Prevents cross-contamination, never share
- Zinc Supplement (15–30 mg) — Supports immune & neurotransmitter balance
- Magnesium Supplement (200–400 mg) — Reduces muscle tension, supports heart rhythm
- Electrolytes — Maintains hydration and mineral balance
- Disinfecting Wipes — Clean surfaces before use
- Credit-Card Plates (x2) — Use dedicated clean surfaces, never banknotes
- Condoms & Lubricant — Protection and comfort during intimacy

**Care Alarms (Settings)**
User configures notifications for the night:
- INTAKE LIMIT (Logged Units)
- TARGET DEPARTURE TIME
- BREATHING BREAKS (Rest Intervals)
- HYDRATION SYNC (Water Reminder Frequency)

---

### PHASE 2: ACTION (Living the Experience)

**Grounding Tool (The Pulse)**
- A pulsing aura visualization in the screen center. 
- Side icons trigger:
  1. Love Breath (Breathing techniques)
  2. Love Vision (Interactive grounding visualizations)
  3. Love Letters (Quick voice/text notes during the action)

**Friends Pulse (Circle of Love)**
- A simplified view of the user’s selected circle of friends.
- Minimalist check-in: "I am present" / "I am here".
- Avoid the word "safe". Focus on "Pulse" and "Connection".

**The Set (Laboratory)**
- A functional tool to log units/substances taken.
- A redosing interval timer based on the "Care Alarms" frequency.
- A factual, quick-reference guide for dangerous combinations (Something to Remember).

**Lock Screen Care Notifications**
- Active push notifications during the festival:
- "Time to hydrate. Honor your body."
- "Breathing break. Take a moment for Love Breath."
- "Rest interval. Find a place to sit, enjoy a banana or chocolate."

---

### PHASE 3: ATTENTION (Integration and Reflection)

**Love Letters (The Journal)**
- A space for reflection after the event. 
- Record voice memos or notes about what was felt and learned.

**Create / You Speak (Master Thesis Research)**
- A survey and feedback portal.
- Includes specific questions for the user to help with building the Master's Thesis.
- Space for feature requests and user "votes" on what they want to see next in Prema.
- Intro text: "Your voice helps us grow. This data supports our Master's Thesis research on conscious nightlife."

---

### UI/UX & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Deploy as a mobile-responsive web app or React Native prototype.
- Ensure transitions between Intention, Action, and Attention are smooth and meaningful.
- Disclaimer on first load: Warm and supportive tone. State clearly that the app does not promote drug use but supports those who have already made their choices to stay connected and care for themselves.
- The aura in Phase 2 (Action) should be the visual "heartbeat" of the app.
