Refine the "Breath of Love" screen into a high-end, immersive art experience. The goal is a progressive "UI dissolution" that leads the user from instruction into deep meditation.

  - Only the Title "breath of love" remains at the top (softly glowing).
  - Only the Core Pulsating Sphere/Circle remains in the center.

6. REPLIT / CODE INSTRUCTIONS
- Use Framer Motion or CSS Transitions for all fading and sliding.

The final result must feel like a digital sanctuary—an intersection of high-technology and pure, silent consciousness. No buttons, no numbers, no clutter. Just the user, the light, and the breath.
